About Charge Ghar Rental

Charge Ghar Rental is the official mobile application of a power bank sharing service operating in Nepal. The app lets you find nearby charging stations, rent a portable power bank, and return it at any participating station in the network.

Our service is designed for people who need to charge their phones while away from home, at work, or while traveling.

How It Works





Open the app and view nearby Charge Ghar stations on the map.



Scan the QR code on a station to unlock a power bank.



Use the power bank to charge your device while you go about your day.



Return it to any participating Charge Ghar station when you are done.



Review your rental summary and payment details in the app.

Key Features





Station locator: View participating stations near you, including their location details and power bank availability.



QR code rental: Rent a power bank quickly by scanning the code on the station.



Return at any station: Return the power bank at any participating station in the network, not only the one where you rented it.



Pricing information: Rental rates and applicable charges are shown in the app before you confirm a rental.



Rental history: View past rentals, payment records, and receipts in your account.



Customer support: Contact our support team through the app for help with rentals, payments, or returns.

Our Mission

To provide convenient and affordable access to mobile charging for people in Nepal, while encouraging the shared use of devices instead of individual ownership.

Our Vision

A Nepal where mobile charging is easy to access in everyday places, from Kathmandu to communities across the country.

Sustainability

Shared power banks serve many users instead of one. By encouraging shared use, our model aims to reduce the number of individual power banks purchased and help limit electronic waste.

Community and Partnerships

We work with local businesses and organizations, such as cafés, shopping centers, restaurants, transport hubs, and healthcare facilities, to host stations in convenient public places. Station availability varies by location and is shown in the app.

Why Use Charge Ghar Rental





Convenience: Stations are placed in commonly visited public locations, and the in-app map helps you find the nearest one.



Portability: Carry the power bank with you while you charge, then return it to a participating station.



Sustainability: Shared use supports a reduction in electronic waste.



Transparent pricing: Rates are displayed in the app before you start a rental, with no subscription required to use the service.

Permissions Used by the App

To provide its core features, the app may request the following permissions. Each is used only for the purpose described.





Location: To show nearby stations on the map.



Camera: To scan the QR code on a station when renting a power bank.



Notifications: To send rental, return, and payment updates.



Internet access: To connect with our servers and process rentals.

Privacy and Data

We respect your privacy. Please review our Privacy Policy within the app and on our website to learn how your information is collected, used, and protected.

Contact Us

For questions, feedback, or support, please contact us through the Help section of the app.

Download Charge Ghar Rental to find a station and start your rental.

Charge Ghar Rental Policy
Charge Ghar Pvt. Ltd. · Registration No. 36949/81/82 · PAN 622388100 Tokha, Kathmandu, Nepal · contact@chargeghar.app · chargeghar.app · +977-9857860060
Effective Date: Oct 16, 2026
This Rental Policy sets out how power bank rentals work through the Charge Ghar Rental mobile application (the “App”) and related service. It forms part of, and must be read together with, the Charge Ghar Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. If this Rental Policy conflicts with the Terms and Conditions regarding a rental, this Rental Policy prevails for that rental.
1. Rental Process
Step 1: Find a station. Open the App and use the live map to locate the nearest available station.
Step 2: Scan the QR code. Tap “Rent” and scan the QR code on the station. Ensure your wallet balance is sufficient before renting.
Step 3: Collect your power bank. The slot opens and the power bank is released. The rental timer begins immediately upon release.
Step 4: Charge your device. Use the built-in cables (USB-C, Lightning, Micro-USB). The power bank is portable and may be carried with you during the rental.
Step 5: Return to any station. Insert the power bank into any available slot at any Charge Ghar station. The slot LED turns green on success. Check the App for the “Rental Ended” notification.
2. Fees and Charges
2.1 The actual fee amounts are displayed in the App before you confirm a rental. By confirming, you accept the displayed fees. Fees are in Nepalese Rupees (NPR) and may be subject to applicable taxes.
2.2 Charges are automatically deducted from your Charge Ghar wallet. Wallet top-ups are processed by licensed third-party payment providers; Charge Ghar does not collect or store your card numbers, wallet credentials or PINs.
2.3 The following charges may apply:
Base Fee (first hour): charged from the moment the power bank is released.
Hourly Rate: charged per hour after the first hour.
Daily Cap (24 hours): the maximum charge for any 24-hour period.
Security Deposit: refundable upon successful return (see Section 4).
Overdue Fee: applied after the maximum rental period is exceeded.
Lost Penalty: charged if the power bank is not returned within the period specified in the App (currently NPR 3,000).
3. Overdue and Non-Return
3.1 A power bank is considered Overdue once it has not been returned after the number of hours specified in the App.
3.2 Push notifications are sent before Overdue status is triggered. Enabling notifications is recommended so that you receive these reminders.
3.3 Overdue fees accumulate on top of regular rental fees.
3.4 A power bank not returned within the number of days specified in the App is marked as Lost, and the Lost Penalty is charged immediately.
3.5 The Security Deposit is forfeited on non-return and is applied toward the Lost Penalty; any remaining balance is charged to your wallet or otherwise recovered from you.
3.6 Your account is suspended until the Lost Penalty is paid in full.
3.7 If you return a power bank after it has been marked Lost, Charge Ghar may, at its sole discretion, refund part of the penalty.
4. Returning the Power Bank
4.1 You may return the power bank to any available slot at any Charge Ghar station across Nepal.
4.2 Insert the power bank firmly until it clicks into place.
4.3 The slot LED turns green to confirm a successful return, and the App sends a “Rental Ended” notification with a final bill summary.
4.4 If the slot is full or a technical error occurs, try another slot or a nearby station.
4.5 Do not leave the power bank unattended without completing the digital return in the App. Fees continue to accrue until the return is confirmed.
4.6 The Security Deposit is refunded to your wallet within 24 hours of a confirmed return.
5. Power Bank Care
5.1 Avoid dropping the power bank, exposing it to water, or exposing it to extreme temperatures.
5.2 Do not use a visibly damaged power bank. Report it through the App’s Help section.
5.3 Do not open, disassemble or modify the power bank.
5.4 Intentional damage results in a charge equal to the full replacement cost, as described in the Terms and Conditions.
6. Cancellation
6.1 A rental cannot be cancelled once the power bank has been released from the station.
6.2 To end a rental, return the power bank to any Charge Ghar station.
6.3 If a rental occurred accidentally because of a technical error, contact support within 5 minutes with a screenshot. Verified errors are reversed.
7. Support and Assistance
In-App: Settings → Help & Support → Report an Issue
Email: support@chargeghar.app
Phone: +977-9857860060
Response time: within 2 hours for active rental issues; within 24 hours for general inquiries
Support is available 24/7 for active rental emergencies.
8. Related Policies
Your rental is also governed by the Charge Ghar Terms and Conditions (including liability, damages, account suspension and dispute resolution) and the Privacy Policy (including how rental and location data is handled).
